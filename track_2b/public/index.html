NEW INPUT / LOCAL VALIDATION FIRST
Supply a small policy and form

      
Use only non-sensitive examples. Paste policy prose and a SurveyJS JSON definition, then list the independent driver field IDs. Validate locally checks the exact text on this local server and makes no provider request. Inputs stay local until you explicitly choose Extract below.

      
Supported subset and limits
One page with 5–10 fields, including 1–6 independent drivers placed before all targets. Drivers must be Boolean or radiogroup with 2–8 string choices. Numeric driver questions are unsupported: integer bounds and step:1 do not guarantee exact integer answers in SurveyJS. Targets may additionally use text with inputType number and integer min/max bounds at most 20 apart. Numeric target answers are not checked. At most 256 driver combinations (the product of the independent driver domain sizes). Only the validator’s narrow property and condition grammar is accepted. Dynamic panels, calculations, scripts, remote choices and unsupported properties are rejected. URLs are never fetched and pasted content is never rendered as HTML.
Independent drivers are always-visible inputs whose availability cannot depend on other answers. The checker needs every combination to be reachable; dependent drivers would invalidate that assumption.
Policy: at most 12,000 UTF-16 code units and 16,384 UTF-8 bytes. Form JSON: 32,768 UTF-8 bytes, 16 nesting levels and 2,048 values. Driver list: 512 UTF-8 bytes. Form and question titles: 400 characters each. Question IDs start with a lowercase letter and use lowercase letters, digits or underscores (51 characters maximum). Choice values use letters, digits, spaces, underscores or hyphens (60 characters maximum). Oversized or unsupported inputs are rejected rather than shortened.
